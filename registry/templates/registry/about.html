> about

A community-powered database for Linux hardware compatibility. Find out if your laptop, GPU, WiFi card, or other hardware works on Linux before you buy.

[1] how it works
1.Users submit compatibility reports for their hardware + Linux distro combination.
2.Maintainers review and approve reports to ensure quality.
3.Community members vote on reports and add driver fixes.
4.Everyone benefits from a growing database of verified compatibility data.
[2] compatibility ratings
Gold — Everything works out of the box
Silver — Most things work, minor issues
Bronze — Usable with significant workarounds
Broken — Does not boot or unusable
[3] tech stack

Built with Django, MySQL, Tailwind CSS, and HTMX. Open source and community-driven.
