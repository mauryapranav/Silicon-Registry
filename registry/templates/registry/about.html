About Silicon Registry

Silicon Registry is a crowdsourced database for tracking Linux hardware compatibility across laptops, desktops, and individual components.

How Reports Work

Every report submitted by the community gets assigned an overall boot status. Here is what they mean:

  • Gold Flawless out-of-the-box experience. Everything works without any tweaking or manual intervention.
  • Silver Works well, but requires minor tweaks (e.g., adding a kernel parameter, installing a specific driver).
  • Bronze Boots and functions, but has persistent issues with non-critical components (e.g., fingerprint reader, webcam).
  • Broken Critical issues. Fails to boot, kernel panics, or core functionality (Wi-Fi, display) is completely broken.

The Trust System

To ensure high-quality data, we use a community trust system:

+5
Report Approved
+10
Driver Fix Accepted
-15
Spam Penalty

Your Trust Ratio is calculated based on your positive and negative points. Users with a high trust ratio gain additional privileges in the community.
